All-Weather Bike / AWB

The joy of riding.
Fewer compromises.

Some journeys are too short for a car. And too good to give up because of the weather. AWB began with a simple idea: keep what we love about cycling and rethink what everyday riding still needs. Our three-wheeled concept brings together pedal power, electric assistance and adjustable weather protection in a compact vehicle designed for everyday journeys.
