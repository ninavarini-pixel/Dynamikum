01 / Tilting

Natural cornering.

02 / Steering

A tighter turn.

Rear-wheel steering is being explored to support a target turning diameter of 3.4 metres.

03 / Assistance

Power when needed.

Electric assistance to complement pedal power and support everyday mobility.

Lightweight thinking

Less weight.
More freedom.

The original project targets a low vehicle mass and the use of standard bicycle wheels enabled by its innovative tilting concept.

The deck cites a target of 30 kg without batteries. This is not a verified finished-product weight.
