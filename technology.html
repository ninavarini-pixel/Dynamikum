02.1 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

Every component
has a reason.

The technical ambition is a lighter, more maneuverable and more enjoyable all-weather bicycle.

01

Tilting architecture

The tilting mechanism is designed to create a natural carving sensation in corners while enabling the use of conventional bicycle wheels.

02

Rear-wheel steering

Fixed front wheels and rear steering are central to the concept's target 3.4 m turning diameter and simplified front-end maintenance.

03

Electric assistance

Electric support complements pedaling. The pitch deck explores a modular battery concept based on multiple small Einhell batteries.

04

Adaptive enclosure

An adjustable canopy is intended to provide variable protection from rain, sun and wind without sacrificing the openness of cycling.

02.2 / ENGINEERING TARGET

Weight is
a design decision.

The pitch deck describes an ambitious target of 30 kg for the empty vehicle without batteries. It is a development objective, not a tested specification.
