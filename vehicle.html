01 / Vehicle & Technology

Purpose in
every detail.

AWB brings together a compact protective cabin, bicycle-based mobility and electric assistance in one three-wheel development concept.

The vehicle

Designed for the city.

A weather-protected platform that aims to stay light, maneuverable and practical in urban environments.

The technology

Engineering that matters.

The mechanical architecture is designed around natural cornering, a compact turning circle and human power complemented by electric assistance.

Concept visualization. Mechanical configuration and components are under development.

Tilting chassis

Designed to provide a natural cornering sensation and support lightweight bicycle components.

Rear-wheel steering

A central feature of the concept, developed with a target turning diameter of 3.4 metres.

Electric assistance

Electric support is intended to complement pedaling during everyday travel.

Adjustable canopy

Weather protection that can adapt to different riding conditions.

Development targets

Compact by design.

78 cmTarget width
3.4 mTarget turning diameter
≈1 m²Target upright storage area

Values are targets from the project materials, not confirmed production specifications.
