Mobility, reimagined

Movement,
thought through.

Good design isn't only about how something looks. It's about how it works, how it responds and how it feels. AWB explores how a bicycle-like riding experience can meet thoughtful engineering and adaptable protection.

01 / The idea

Every detail has a reason.

The shape, proportions and technical layout of AWB are parts of one design. Ergonomics, mechanics and visual character need to work together, rather than compete for space.

02 / The experience

Open to the ride. Ready for the weather.

Sometimes you want to feel the air around you. Sometimes you'd rather keep the wind and rain outside. The adjustable canopy is designed to offer that choice.

Shelter on your terms.

03 / The engineering

Engineering that follows the movement.

A tilting chassis, rear-wheel steering, electric assistance and an adjustable canopy are designed to work as one. Each addresses a different part of the riding experience; each still needs careful validation.

Naturally into the turn

The tilting mechanism aims to let AWB lean into corners, retaining a familiar part of the cycling experience.

Room to manoeuvre

Rear-wheel steering is intended to help AWB turn and manoeuvre where space is limited.

Your energy, with a little help

Pedalling remains central. Electric assistance is intended to support the rider, not replace the feeling of moving under your own power.

Adjustable weather protection

The canopy is designed to offer a more open ride or additional shelter as conditions change.

04 / Design details

A closer look at the thinking.

From lighting to wheel mechanisms, the details reveal how AWB's form and function are being developed together.

05 / Development targets

Designed to take up less space.

The concept aims to make movement and storage easier where every centimetre counts. The figures are historical design targets, not verified production specifications.

From a vision to a tested solution.

AWB is still in development. Prototypes and testing will help determine its final form. This is where the right engineering partners can make a real difference.
