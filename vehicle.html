01 / Vehicle & Technology

The freedom of a bike.
The comfort of more.

Meet AWB — an all-weather, electric-assist three-wheeler developed around a simple idea: make everyday urban travel feel more practical, in more conditions.

01 / The idea
A different kind of everyday mobility

Between the bicycle
and the car.

A bicycle is wonderfully direct. A car offers shelter. AWB explores the space between them: a compact, pedal-powered vehicle with electric assistance and an adjustable protective canopy.

Three wheels, a narrow footprint and an enclosed riding position are brought together in one urban mobility concept.

02 / The experience
Designed around the rider

Protection without
losing the ride.

The adjustable canopy is intended to respond to changing conditions. Inside, the concept keeps the rider at the centre of the experience — with human power complemented by electric assistance.

Inside AWB

Built around everyday use.

A compact seating environment and weather protection are considered together rather than as separate add-ons.

03 / The engineering

Small footprint.
Considered mechanics.

The development concept combines a tilting chassis, rear-wheel steering and electric assistance. Each system is being explored in service of urban manoeuvrability and a natural riding feel.

Tilting chassis

Intended to support a natural cornering sensation while drawing on lightweight bicycle architecture.

Rear-wheel steering

A distinctive part of the concept, developed around a target turning diameter of 3.4 metres.

Electric assistance

Designed to work alongside pedalling rather than replace the rider's input.

Adjustable canopy

Protection designed to adapt as riding conditions change.

04 / Design details

The whole is in
the details.

Lighting, wheel assemblies and mechanical packaging are all part of the ongoing design study.
