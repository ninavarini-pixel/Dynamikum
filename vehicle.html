03 / The engineering

Smart thinking, simply explained.

At the heart of AWB is an innovative leaning mechanism designed to make cornering feel natural. It's a distinctive approach in this category of three-wheeled cycles — and one we're continuing to refine, alongside other improvements to the riding experience.

Natural cornering

The frame is designed to lean into turns, for a feeling closer to riding a bicycle.

Room to manoeuvre

Steering from the rear aims to make turning around easier, even on narrow streets.

A welcome boost

The motor is intended to lend a hand when pedalling gets demanding.

Weather on your terms

The roof is designed to adapt to changing conditions, without turning the ride into a closed cabin.
