Mobility, reimagined

A different kind
of city vehicle.

AWB combines pedalling, electric assistance and an adjustable canopy in a compact three-wheel concept. Here is how the design is intended to work.

01 / The idea

The idea: a middle ground.

AWB explores a middle ground between a bicycle and a car: human-powered movement with added assistance and protection, without the footprint of a conventional car.

02 / The experience

The ride: open to the city.

An adjustable canopy aims to offer protection when needed. Pedalling remains central, with electric assistance intended to support the rider.

Built around everyday use.

03 / The engineering

The mechanics behind the ride.

A tilting chassis is being explored for natural-feeling turns; rear-wheel steering targets tight manoeuvres, while electric assistance supports pedalling. These systems are still in development.

Tilting chassis

Intended to support a natural cornering sensation while drawing on lightweight bicycle architecture.

Rear-wheel steering

A distinctive part of the concept, developed around a target turning diameter of 3.4 metres.

Electric assistance

Designed to work alongside pedalling rather than replace the rider's input.

Adjustable canopy

Protection designed to adapt as riding conditions change.
