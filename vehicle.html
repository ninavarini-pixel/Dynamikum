Mobility, reimagined

Engineered around
the way you ride.

Meet AWB: a fresh take on everyday city travel. Three wheels, pedal power and electric support — with a roof that adjusts to the weather.

01 / The idea

A new kind of independence.

For the commute, the quick errand or the long way home. AWB offers a more comfortable alternative to the everyday trips that don't need a car.

02 / The experience

A chassis designed to lean.

Fresh air when you want it. More shelter when you need it. The ride adapts to the day, not the other way around.

Weather protection that adapts.

03 / The engineering

The mechanics behind AWB.

AWB's distinctive leaning system brings a bicycle-inspired approach to cornering on three wheels. It's a key part of the design — and one we're continually refining to make the ride even better.

Designed to lean

A leaning frame is designed to follow the turn instead of staying rigidly upright.

Steering from the rear

Rear-wheel steering is designed to make tight manoeuvres easier.

Assistance while pedalling

Electric assistance aims to make demanding stretches feel lighter while you stay in control.

Adjustable weather protection

The adjustable roof gives the design flexibility for changing weather, without the feel of a closed cabin.
