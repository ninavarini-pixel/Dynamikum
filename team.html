01 / 1 position · 5–10 years of experience

Senior Design Engineer

Lead mechanical design work on a new all-weather electric-assist velomobile, helping translate an ambitious mobility concept into robust engineering solutions.

View more

What you'll work on

  • Develop and refine mechanical assemblies and parametric CAD models for the AWB concept.
  • Work with the project team to evaluate design choices, feasibility and manufacturability.
  • Support integration of the chassis, steering, canopy and other mechanical subsystems.
  • Contribute to technical reviews, prototype preparation and engineering problem-solving.

What we're looking for

  • Approximately 5–10 years of relevant mechanical design or product development experience.
  • Strong practical skills in SolidWorks or a comparable parametric CAD system.
  • Ability to work independently on complex mechanical assemblies and communicate design decisions.
  • Experience turning designs into physical prototypes is valuable.

This role is intended for an experienced engineer rather than a graduate.

Working arrangement

Hybrid collaboration, initially around one day per week in Vráble. During prototype assembly, on-site work may increase to approximately three days per week or a full-week assembly block. Exact employment terms and compensation will be discussed individually.

CAD & Simulation Engineer

Help develop and evaluate the AWB mechanical concept through CAD modeling, engineering analysis and simulation.

View more

What you'll work on

  • Create and iterate parametric 3D parts and assemblies.
  • Support mechanical analyses and simulation-based design decisions.
  • Explore design alternatives and document results with the engineering team.
  • Assist with preparation and evaluation of prototype components.

What we're looking for

  • Interest in mechanical engineering, product development and technical problem-solving.
  • Experience with any suitable CAD system; prior SolidWorks knowledge is welcome but not mandatory.
  • Interest in SolidWorks Simulation and Flow Simulation; previous hands-on experience is an advantage.
  • Motivation to learn. Recent graduates, university students and promising candidates with secondary technical education are welcome.

Experience level is flexible; practical ability and willingness to learn matter.

Working arrangement

Hybrid collaboration, initially around one day per week in Vráble. During prototype assembly, on-site work may increase to approximately three days per week or a full-week assembly block. Exact employment terms and compensation will be discussed individually.

Junior Industrial Designer

Shape the look, usability and product experience of the AWB concept in close collaboration with mechanical engineers.

View more

What you'll work on

  • Develop and communicate product design concepts for the vehicle and its details.
  • Build and revise parametric 3D models to explore design directions.
  • Collaborate with engineers on practical constraints and design integration.
  • Support design iterations as the concept progresses toward prototyping.

What we're looking for

  • Interest in industrial or transportation product design.
  • Ability to work with parametric 3D design software; no specific application is required.
  • Ability to communicate ideas clearly through sketches, models or a portfolio.
  • Willingness to work collaboratively across design and engineering.

A specific software package and years-of-experience requirement have not been prescribed.
