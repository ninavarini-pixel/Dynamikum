Progress through collaboration

Good ideas grow
through collaboration.

A new vehicle concept raises new questions. Some can be answered on the drawing board; others only when the first parts are built and tested. AWB needs engineering validation, prototype testing and practical manufacturing solutions.

Where experience meets opportunity.

We believe the strongest solutions emerge when different kinds of expertise meet. Three areas matter especially for AWB's next steps:

Engineering & prototyping

Help turn mechanical concepts into assemblies that can be built, tested and improved.

Manufacturing & supply

Bring expertise in materials, components and production processes that can shape a feasible vehicle.

Mobility & distribution

Help explore future market entry and distribution, with an initial focus on Austria and Germany.
